# Largo IA

Marketing site for Largo IA, a French generative-AI training organisation. 100% remote, jargon-free, and built to be AI-Act and GDPR aware.

## Who it is for

Owners and teams of French small and mid-size businesses (artisans, shops, agencies, small industry) who have a business to run, little time, and real apprehension about AI. They are not developers and do not want to become one. They want someone calm and credible who shows them, over video, what AI concretely changes for them from the first week.

## The promise

"Prendre le large avec l'IA": move from worry to a working use of AI.

## Stack

Next.js with MDX content, Drizzle ORM on Neon Postgres, Auth.js, GSAP for motion.

## Run

```bash
pnpm install
pnpm dev   # http://localhost:3000
```

See `docs/` and `PRODUCT.md` for the brand and product direction.
